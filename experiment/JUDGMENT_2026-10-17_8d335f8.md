## A. N6 — the row's wording, corrected (Q1, final)

The external review of the paper draft made a point that applies to the STATUS row as I worded it in 934fbae: "detectable by residual
direction without labels" claims more than the measurement supports. Two corrections:
1. The clustering of residuals by hidden filler (same/different cosine 0.19 / 0.05) is label-free structure, but it may come from the hidden
   token's *identity vector* being present in the residual at a consistent address — which is exactly what 0.963 at the reader's address
   shows — and not from anything about the *relation* "recipient". The 1-NN at 0.57 names the filler through the neighbours' labels; it is a
   separability diagnostic, not label-free recovery.
2. The claim the row may carry is therefore: "the identity of a role omitted from the legend remains in the residual at a consistent address;
   recoverable at 0.963 once the question is named and its address calibrated on 120 labelled sentences; residuals separable by filler
   (1-NN 0.57 with neighbours' labels, chance 0.08); the residual's share does not signal the omission (15, 15-bis: the comparison class fits
   worse); the oracle axis ill-posed by construction (rule 30)". The claim "a missing question is detectable" is NOT made; what would make
   it — the same fillers in several roles, generalisation to new fillers, and a proposal step that names the question — is the next
   measurement if the legend loop needs it, and is written as PLANNED, low priority, with that criterion.
The positioning document gets the sentence of point 2, not the one of 934fbae. 15-bis's reading stands; the share stays one number in the tool.
